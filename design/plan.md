# Crownwake — Prototype Design

## Experience

The player feels like a growing island sovereign because every banner-led procession paints the world in its colour and every defeated rival’s army rises into its wake.

## Profile

- Solo, real-time, continuous 3D world with pause-at-will strategic overview.
- Desktop and mobile browsers; mouse/touch first, keyboard and gamepad alternatives.
- Click or tap to move. Servants trail the master and engage automatically.
- Short campaign session with authored regions, escalating rival formations, territorial setbacks, and a clear prototype victory.
- Engagement comes from positioning, accumulation, discovery, and visible world transformation.

## Core Loop

1. Move across the revealed frontier.
2. Encounter a rival master and its servants.
3. Position the swarm while combat resolves automatically.
4. Defeat every servant and the rival master.
5. Resurrect the defeated servants in the player faction; leave the rival master as a tombstone.
6. Claim the region, reveal adjacent fog, and choose the next frontier from the strategic overview.
7. On defeat, retreat while the victorious rival counterattacks into one adjacent unfortified region.

## Prototype Boundaries

- Simple softly faceted figures remain the prototype characters; formation motion, banners, materials, VFX, and lighting carry the game feel.
- The strategic map supports frontier selection and clearly presents fortification as a future/locked action; its resource economy is intentionally deferred.
- Tombstones are permanent world-history markers without gameplay effects.
- One compact campaign demonstrates onboarding, recruitment, a setback, recovery, and victory.

## Style Formula

Clean minimalist 3D island-diorama rendering with softly faceted geometry, subtle hand-painted surfaces, and restrained sketch-like edges. Rounded compact silhouettes cluster beneath tall, flowing faction banners. Environments use chalk-white cliffs, pale sage grass, misty blue-grey water, and desaturated foliage; player forces use clear turquoise and blue, enemies use coral, yellow, and violet. Calm storybook atmosphere with diffuse overcast light and gentle haze. High faction contrast, uncluttered terrain, readable formations, and consistent three-quarter isometric presentation.

## Formation Language

- Travel: servants compress into a dense banner-led procession behind their master.
- Approach: both armies fan into shallow opposing arcs before contact.
- Combat: servants spread into readable local skirmishes while maintaining faction cohesion.
- Conversion: the defeated formation rises together, changes colour, converges on the player banner, then settles into the larger travelling cluster.

## Controls

- Mouse/touch: click or tap ground to move; map button toggles the strategic overview.
- Keyboard: physical `WASD`/arrow codes move, `Space` toggles map, `Escape` closes it.
- Gamepad: left stick moves, south button toggles map.
- No drag controls.
